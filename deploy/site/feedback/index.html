Feedback and beta testing

Thought Coach is young, and it gets better from what players tell it. Feedback on a single card in a story goes through the Feedback on this card button on that card; use this page for everything else.

Become a beta tester

  1. Set up (about two minutes) and play three rapid or classical games with it.
  2. On each story, use Feedback on this card wherever a card is right, wrong, or misses something you did think but didn't draw or say: that is the most useful thing a tester can do.
  3. Then tell us below what was confusing, what you'd want next, and whether you'd keep using it. Choose "Beta tester".
