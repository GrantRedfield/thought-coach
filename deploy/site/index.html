Review how you think,
not just what you played.

Draw arrows for the moves you consider and think out loud while you play on lichess. After the game, Thought Coach checks your thinking against Stockfish: the threats you saw and missed, the moves you never looked at, how you felt, and the moment the game turned.

Sign in with lichess How to set it up

Free and open source. Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer.

⏱
Longer games work best

Play 10+5 or 15+10 rapid: there's time to draw your ideas and say them. In bullet there's no room to think out loud, and moves played with under 30 seconds left aren't judged on your thinking.

🎙
Give it a few games

Drawing arrows and talking while you play feels strange at first, and you may be a little slower for a few games. That passes quickly. Start by drawing their threats, then add your own moves, then talk. Your first games, step by step

How it works

You play. It listens. Stockfish checks. You get the story.

Nothing appears during the game. Everything happens after lichess says it's over.
