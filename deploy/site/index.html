Four questions, in the order strong players ask them

Each review checks which of these show up in your recorded thinking, and which don't.

1

What does your opponent want?

Their threat or plan, before you choose your move.

2

Where are the weaknesses?

Loose pawns, holes and an exposed king, on both sides.

3

What are your candidates?

Two or three real options, so the best one can be on the list.

4

Which piece is doing least?

Improving your worst piece is often the best quiet move.

Game Story

A chess lesson from your own game.

A few minutes after each game, Thought Coach turns it into a short story you tap through, like a coach going over the game with you.

  1. The moments that mattered, chosen from the game, not every move.
  2. Your recorded thinking at each one: the arrows you drew and what you said.
  3. What went well and what didn't, checked by Stockfish.
  4. One focus for your next game, with the moment that shows why.

Training focus for the next game

Answer the threat you saw. When you've drawn their threat, play it on the board in your head after your chosen move: does your move still hold?

After you pick up a focus, your next games are checked to see whether that habit is changing, with how sure that is, so one lucky game isn't called progress.

Built for long-term improvement

Each game adds to your profile, and your next focus comes from what keeps repeating.

  1. PlayA rapid or classical game on lichess, with your thinking recorded.
  2. AnalyzeYour Game Story and full review, a few minutes after the game.
  3. TrainOne focus, plus practice positions from your own games.
  4. MeasureYour next games show whether that habit is changing.
  5. RepeatThe profile sharpens with every game you add.

Three steps to your first review

  1. Connect

    Sign in with lichess and install the free Chrome extension.

  2. Play

    Record your thinking with arrows for the moves you consider, and voice if you like.

  3. Improve

    After the game, review your decisions and get coaching on your thinking.

  • Voice recording is optional
  • No move suggestions or engine help during games
