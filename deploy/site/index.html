Thought Coach

Your games Setup

Review how you think,
not just what you played.

Draw arrows for the moves you consider and think out loud while you play on lichess. When the game ends, Thought Coach checks your thinking against Stockfish: the threats you saw and missed, the candidates you never looked at, and the moment the game turned.

Sign in with lichess How to set it up

Free and open source. Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer.

01

Play as usual

The extension quietly records the arrows you draw, the pieces you pick up and (if you want) your voice.

02

Reviewed after the game

A few minutes after it ends, Stockfish and a speech model go through your thinking, move by move.

03

One thing to work on

A short story of your game, the questions you skipped, and a single focus for your next game.

Fair play. Nothing is shown during a game: the extension only records. A review starts only once lichess says the game is over, and only for games you played.

Privacy. Signing in only asks lichess for your username: no password, no access to your account (we hand the sign-in back to lichess straight away). Voice recordings are deleted once they are transcribed. Your review pages aren't listed anywhere, but anyone you give a link to can open them.
